The European GNU Radio Days will be held in Rennes, France, from November 16 to November 18, 2026.

The event aims to bring together the European SDR community around scientific presentations, 
technical feedback, tutorials, demonstrations, educational materials for students, and 
discussions on the evolution of open radio technologies.

This new edition of the European GNU Radio Days will focus on **cybersecurity with SDR**. 
Other topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

*   GNU Radio, including GNU Radio 4 evolution
*   Wireless communications and future networks, including 5G and 6G and SATCOM
*   Signal processing and AI for radio systems
*   Spectrum monitoring
*   Radar, sensing, and localization
*   RF cybersecurity, side-channel analysis and TEMPEST
*   Open-source radio ecosystems
*   Rapid prototyping, testbeds and experimental platforms

See [the call for contributions](https://gnuradiodays.sciencesconf.org/resource/page/id/8).
